How it works

  • Hazard gate: slope, 5×5 roughness and a full landing-ellipse check must pass before a cell can score at all.
  • Illumination: horizon ray-marching at a low polar Sun (2° elevation) across 8 azimuths gives the fraction of the lunar day a cell is lit.
  • Comms: line-of-sight to Earth at a low elevation angle, averaged over ±10° of azimuth libration.
  • Science: proximity to permanently shadowed regions, the cold traps where water ice can persist.
  • Explainable ranking: each site shows exactly why it scored as it did, so planners can trust and audit the pick.

Real-data path: swap the synthetic grid for LRO LOLA 5 m/px south-pole DEMs and LROC imagery; the same pipeline applies unchanged. Candidate regions follow NASA Artemis south-polar site studies.
